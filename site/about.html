About the World History Map

An open, community-edited map of the world from about 3000 BCE to today. Move the slider to see borders at 14 moments, and explore the people, works, inventions and environmental events of each period. Click a state to see its dynasties and rulers, or follow one modern country through time. Every record cites a source, and every change is reviewed before it goes live.

Known limitations

  • Borders are approximate. There are only 14 snapshots, so a state can appear or vanish between two of them. Dashed outlines are the least certain; purple outlines are sourced corrections (details).
  • Land with no named realm is shown pale and keeps its last recorded realm; many early areas have none.
  • Follow a country uses 110 m outlines, so small countries and islands are missing. Shares are an automatic area match; only ten countries have curated, sourced notes (marked with a star).
  • Rulers, dynasties and curated lineage notes cover a limited set of states, and many reign years and notes were entered from secondary sources and are not yet independently reviewed.
  • Coverage is uneven. Imported lists favour people with many Wikipedia articles, which skews towards Europe; this is being corrected through curated lists and contributions.
  • Disputed dates (for example the Thera eruption) are shown as disputed, not decided. Environmental events are described, never explained by cause.
  • The data quality report lists open flags: data-quality.md.
